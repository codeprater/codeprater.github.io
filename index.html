    About Prater Apps

    Prater Apps LLC is an independent studio in Eastpointe, Michigan, run by Courtney Prater. We build iPhone apps for real, everyday moments: tools that help people communicate, stay safe and get work done, and games that bring people together.

    Many of our apps are made for people who are often left out of mainstream technology: nonspeaking and minimally verbal people, their families and caregivers, and neighbors in Detroit and beyond.
