About

Prater Apps LLC is a Michigan limited liability company founded by Courtney Prater, a Detroit-area iOS developer who spent 25 years in commercial construction before teaching herself to build apps.

Our focus is simple: tools that give people a voice, keep families safe, and are genuinely fun to use, built natively for Apple devices with accessibility from the first line of code.
