    About Prater Apps

    Prater Apps LLC is an independent studio in Eastpointe, Michigan, founded by Courtney Prater. Our focus is communication technology for nonspeaking and minimally verbal people and the people who support them.

    Courtney has built and shipped 18 apps to the App Store as a solo founder, across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and CarPlay. YAPPP is where that work comes together.
