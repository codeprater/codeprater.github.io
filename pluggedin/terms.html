1. Who we are

PluggedIn connects people who need help with everyday tasks ("Plugs") with independent people who do that work ("PluggedIn workers"). PluggedIn is a technology platform. We do not perform jobs, we do not employ PluggedIn workers, and we do not supervise or control how a job is done.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old, able to form a binding contract, and use your real identity. You may hold one account. You are responsible for everything done with your account.

3. PluggedIn workers are independent contractors

PluggedIn workers choose which jobs to bid on, set their own bids, provide their own tools and transportation, and decide how to do the work. Nothing in these Terms creates an employment, agency, partnership, or joint-venture relationship between PluggedIn and any user. PluggedIn workers are responsible for their own taxes, licenses, and insurance.

4. Posting and accepting jobs

Plugs must describe each job honestly, including its location, scope, hazards, and any pets or people present, and must record a video or photos showing exactly what needs to be done before the job can go out for bids. A PluggedIn worker must review that video or those photos and agree to the task in the app before any work begins. The recorded video or photos, the written description and the agreed price together define the work. Changes to scope or price must be agreed in the app.

5. Prohibited jobs

You may not use PluggedIn for illegal activity, weapons, drugs, sexual services, childcare, medical care, hazardous waste, or work that legally requires a license (such as electrical, plumbing, or gas work). We may remove any job or account that breaks this rule.

6. Payments happen only in the app

All payments go through PluggedIn with Apple Pay. No cash, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, or any other off-platform payment. Asking for or accepting off-platform payment is grounds for removal. When a job is posted, the Plug is charged the job price, a 5% service fee, and any tip. The money is held until the Plug approves the work, then the PluggedIn worker receives the job price minus a 5% service fee, plus any tip, through our payment partner, Stripe. PluggedIn workers must complete Stripe's payout setup to be paid.

7. Cancellations, refunds, and disputes

A Plug may cancel before work starts for a refund, less any cancellation fee shown in the app. Once a PluggedIn worker has arrived or started, refunds are handled through the in-app dispute process. If a Plug does not approve or dispute submitted proof within 24 hours, PluggedIn may release payment to the PluggedIn worker. Our decision on a dispute is final as far as the payment is concerned, but does not limit your other legal rights.

8. Verification and background checks

We verify identity and, for PluggedIn workers who choose it, request a background check from a third-party screening company. These checks are limited, rely on outside records, and do not guarantee anyone's identity, history, behavior, or skill. Always use good judgment and the safety tools in the app.

9. Ratings and content

Ratings, messages, and photos must be honest and respectful. You give PluggedIn permission to store and show content you upload as needed to run the service, resolve disputes, and keep users safe.

10. Safety

PluggedIn workers must wear a high-visibility safety vest the entire time they are on a job site and check in with a photo wearing it before starting work. PluggedIn workers must send photos or video of the finished work as proof before payout. If anyone is in danger, call 911 first. Stop work immediately if a job becomes unsafe or goes beyond what was shown and described. Report problems in the app.

Our total liability for any claim is limited to the greater of the fees PluggedIn received from you for the job involved or $100. Some places do not allow these limits, so they may not apply to you.

13. Indemnity

14. Ending your account
