How disputes work

  1. Open it in the app. On the job, tap Review the finished work, then Not done right, open a dispute. Payment is frozen right away and automatic release stops.
  2. Add details here. Tell us what happened. Both the Plug and the PluggedIn worker can add details and extra photos.
  3. We review the record. Support compares the before and after photos and video, the vest check-in, time on site, the agreed task and the job's messages. Most cases are settled within 3 business days.
  4. Money goes where it should. If the work wasn't done, the Plug gets a full or partial refund to the card in Apple Pay they paid with. If it was done as agreed, the PluggedIn worker is paid.

Add details to your case
