Cash Policy

Why every PluggedIn job is paid in the app, and what happens if it isn’t.

On this page

  1. The rule
  2. Why
  3. What counts as off-platform
  4. What happens if it occurs
  5. Fees, plainly

The rule

No cash. No Venmo. No Zelle. Apple Pay only. Every PluggedIn job is paid through the app, start to finish, including tips.

Why

In-app payment protects both sides. Plugs get refunds when a job is cancelled before work starts, and a dispute process if something goes wrong. PluggedIn workers are guaranteed payment for approved work, and pay releases automatically if a Plug doesn’t respond within 24 hours of proof. None of that is possible when money changes hands outside the app.

What counts as off-platform

  • Asking for or offering cash, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, PayPal, gift cards or any other payment outside PluggedIn.
  • Agreeing to “finish the job off the app” or to skip the app next time.
  • Sharing payment handles, bank details or phone numbers to arrange payment.

What happens if it occurs

Report it from the job (Report or Safety center). We review every report. Accounts that ask for or take off-platform payments can be warned, suspended or removed, and lose access to PluggedIn’s refund, dispute and payment protection for that job.

Fees, plainly

Plugs pay the job price plus a 5% service fee. PluggedIn workers receive the job price minus a 5% service fee, plus 100% of tips. Example: a $60 job costs the Plug $63.00, the PluggedIn worker keeps $57.00, and PluggedIn keeps $6.00 before payment processing costs. If a Plug cancels a paid job, PluggedIn keeps a cancellation fee and refunds the rest: $1.50 if no PluggedIn worker has been matched yet, or the 5% service fee (at least $1.50) after one has. The app shows the exact refund before you cancel.

Cashing out

PluggedIn workers cash out to their bank from Wallet. Standard transfers are free and usually arrive in 1–2 business days. Instant cash-out to an eligible debit card arrives in minutes for a 1.5% fee (at least $0.50), shown before you confirm. Payouts are handled by Stripe.
