Who can join

Anyone 18 or older with a valid driver's license or state ID. You sign in with Apple and take a photo of your ID; only the face photo is kept as your avatar.

Find jobs

Swipe through open jobs near you, TikTok-style. Each one plays the Plug's own video or photos so you know exactly what's needed, with the pay, what you keep after fees, the distance and the expected time.

Bid or accept

Accept at the posted price or bid lower with a short message. AI can suggest a fair bid and write the message for you. The Plug sees your profile, stars and reviews with your bid and can accept or decline.

Agree, then go

When you're picked, review the Plug's video and agree to the task. Once a Plug accepts your bid, your location and arrival time are shared with that Plug until you submit proof. When you arrive, check in at the address with a photo wearing your high-visibility vest. A vest is required on every job.

Prove the work

When you're done, record photos or video of the finished work, ideally the same views the Plug recorded. Your pay is released when the Plug approves, or automatically 24 hours after you send proof if they don't respond.

What you keep

You keep the job price minus PluggedIn's 5% fee, plus 100% of tips. Example: on a $60 job you keep $57.00.

Cash out

Set up payouts once through Stripe (bank account and identity). Your Wallet shows your available balance, money on the way and a money tracker of every job after fees. Tap Cash out to send it to your bank, free, usually in 1–2 business days.

Your resume

Every finished job and every review is added to your PluggedIn resume: star average, a 5-to-1 star breakdown, jobs completed, hours worked, on-time rate, cancellations, what Plugs say most and an AI-written highlights summary. Only PluggedIn updates the numbers, so Plugs can trust them. Share it as a link to win more work.

Background-checked workers

Complete an optional background check to earn a badge and take premium jobs that usually pay about 30% more.

Cancelling

If you can't make it, cancel and give the job back. It goes back into the feed for someone else. Cancellations show on your resume, so cancel only when you have to.
