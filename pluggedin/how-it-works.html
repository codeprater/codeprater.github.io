How PluggedIn Works

Plugs are the people hiring. Here's every step from recording the job to approving the work.

1. Record the job

Pick a template or create your own job. Then record a video or take photos of exactly what you want done, walking through every area. A job can't go out for bids until it shows the work, so everyone agrees on what “done” means before anyone starts.

2. Let AI tidy it up (optional)

Tap Write it with AI and PluggedIn turns a few words into a clear title, a scope checklist, an expected time and a suggested price range. It also checks the job against our rules before it goes live.

3. Set the time and price

Choose how long the job should take, when a PluggedIn worker can start, and when it must be finished. Pick a worker tier (Rookie, Verified, Pro, Background-checked or a team) and set your price.

4. Pay and send it out for bids

You pay with Apple Pay when you post: the job price plus a 5% service fee and any tip. PluggedIn holds the money. Your job then appears in the PluggedIn worker feed with your video front and center.

5. Review bids like a resume

Every bid shows the PluggedIn worker's profile: photo, ID-verified and background-check badges, star rating, number of reviews, jobs completed, on-time rate, cancellations and recent reviews. Tap through to their full resume. Accept the one you want or decline any you don't. You can also send a job directly to a PluggedIn worker you trust.

6. They agree to the task

Before any work begins, your PluggedIn worker watches your video and agrees to the task, including wearing a high-visibility vest the whole time on site.

7. Follow along live

See your PluggedIn worker on a map while they're on the way. When they arrive they check in at your address with a photo wearing their vest. You'll see “On site and working,” a running timer against the expected time, and you're notified if they leave the site or the job runs long.

8. Approve the proof

Your PluggedIn worker records photos or video of the finished work. AI compares it with what you asked for and lists anything that looks missing. You approve to release payment, or open a dispute. If you don't respond within 24 hours, payment is released automatically.

9. Review and keep the record

Rate your PluggedIn worker with stars, tags and a written review. It goes on their resume for other Plugs to see. Every job stays in your Work log with times, money and a summary.

Cancelling

Before work starts you can cancel for a refund. At any time you can release your PluggedIn worker and the job goes straight back into the feed for others to bid on, with your payment still held.
