Getting started

What is PluggedIn?

PluggedIn connects people who need everyday help (Plugs) with verified local people who do the work (PluggedIn workers). Post a job, pick a PluggedIn worker, follow along live, and pay in the app.

Who can use PluggedIn?

Anyone 18 or older with a valid ID. We're launching in Detroit, MI first.

How do I switch between hiring and earning?

Use the Hire / Earn switch at the top of Home. Your account works both ways.

Posting a job

How do I post a job?

Tap Post a job on Home. Start from a template or write your own, add the address and photos, choose a PluggedIn worker tier, then pay with Apple Pay. The job goes live right away.

Why do I have to record a video or photos?

Every job needs a video or photos of exactly what you want done before it goes out for bids. PluggedIn workers see it before they bid, agree to it before they start, and send proof back the same way, so everyone agrees on the work before payout. Use Create your own job in Templates to write a job from scratch.

When am I charged?

When you post. PluggedIn holds the money and only releases it to your PluggedIn worker after you approve the work. If you cancel before work starts, you get a refund.

Can I send a job to a PluggedIn worker I already know?

Yes. Use Send a job on Home to offer an open job to a PluggedIn worker you've hired before, or share the job link.

What jobs aren't allowed?

Anything illegal, weapons, drugs, sexual services, childcare, medical care, hazardous waste, and work that legally needs a license, like electrical, plumbing or gas work.

How do I check a PluggedIn worker before hiring?

Tap View resume on any bid to see their stars, reviews from other Plugs, work history and badges. You can also send a job directly to a PluggedIn worker from their resume.

Earning

How do I find jobs?

Switch Home to Earn to see open jobs near you on a map and in a list. Bid on a job or accept it at the posted price.

What do I need on the job?

A high-visibility safety vest, worn the whole time you're on site. When you arrive, you'll check in with a photo of yourself wearing it, then work starts. When you're done, record photos or video of the finished work as proof.

How does the job feed work?

Tap Swipe through jobs on Home in Earn mode. Swipe up to see the next job. Each one plays the Plug's video or photos so you can see exactly what's needed, then tap Accept or Bid.

What is my PluggedIn resume?

Your resume follows your account. It shows your star rating, every review, completed jobs with short summaries, hours worked, on-time rate and cancellations. Plugs see it before they hire you, and you can share it as a link from Profile → My resume. Only PluggedIn can update the numbers, so they can be trusted.

How much does PluggedIn keep?

PluggedIn keeps 5% of the job price. You keep the rest plus 100% of any tip. Wallet shows every job with the fee already taken out.

How do I cash out?

Set up payouts once in Wallet (Stripe asks for your bank and identity). After a Plug approves your work, the money lands in your PluggedIn balance. Tap Cash out to send it to your bank, free, usually in 1–2 business days.

What if the work isn't done right?

Open Review the finished work on the job and tap Not done right, open a dispute. Payment stays on hold while support reviews it. Add details any time on the Disputes page. If the work wasn't done, you're refunded to the card you paid with in Apple Pay.

What if the Plug doesn't approve?

If the Plug doesn't approve or dispute your proof within 24 hours, PluggedIn releases your pay automatically.

What's a Background-checked PluggedIn worker?

PluggedIn workers who pass a background check get a badge and can take premium jobs that require one. Start a check in Profile → Background check.

Account

Where's my job history?

Profile → Work log lists every job you did or hired for, with times, money after fees, and an AI summary of the work. After a job, leave a review from there.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → Delete account. You'll confirm with Apple. Payment records we're required to keep for taxes are retained.

How do I refer a friend?

Profile → Refer a PluggedIn worker gives you a code and an invite link to share.

Tracking and safety

How does live tracking work?

As soon as you accept a PluggedIn worker's bid, you see them on a live map with their arrival time, like a ride app, until they submit proof. PluggedIn workers' locations are never shared outside an active job.

What if something goes wrong?

Call 911 if anyone is in danger. Then use Safety center or Get help on the job to reach PluggedIn support. You can report and block anyone.

Why can't I pay cash?

No cash. No Venmo. No Zelle. Apple Pay only. In-app payment protects both sides: Plugs get refunds and disputes, PluggedIn workers are guaranteed payment for approved work.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account, then confirm with Apple. This deletes your profile, open jobs and bids. If you can't open the app, email courtney@praterapps.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you. Payment records we're required to keep for tax purposes are retained.
