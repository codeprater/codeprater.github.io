Who we are

PluggedIn is an app and website operated by Prater Apps LLC, a Michigan company founded by Courtney Prater (“PluggedIn,” “we,” “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Contact: courtney@praterapps.com.

What we collect from you

  • Account: your name and the email Apple shares with us when you use Sign in with Apple (this may be a private relay address), and an account ID.
  • Age and identity: your date of birth and the photo of your driver’s license or state ID that you choose. PluggedIn's on-device AI crops a face-only photo from it, checks that no text from the card is left, and uses that face as your avatar. That face is the only part of your ID other people ever see. The full ID photo is private: it is stored on your iPhone with file protection and in a private folder in our cloud storage that only your account can open, so it can't be seen by other users. PluggedIn support opens it only to settle a dispute or a safety report about you. You can see or replace it in Profile → My ID on file.
  • Arrival check-in: when a PluggedIn worker arrives at a job, they take a selfie wearing their high-visibility vest. The Plug on that job sees it next to the worker's ID face to confirm it's the same person before work can start.
  • Jobs: job titles, descriptions, addresses, photos and video of the work site, prices, tips, bids, ratings, proof photos, reports and support requests. When you add a photo or video from your library instead of the camera, PluggedIn reads the date and location saved in it to check that it was taken at the job recently; the result is saved with the job.
  • Disputes, reports and blocks: the reason you give, photos and statements you add to a dispute, reports you file about another user, and the list of people you block. Messages in a job are paused while its dispute is open.
  • Messages: chats between a Plug and a PluggedIn worker about a job. These are private to the two of them.
  • Public discussion: questions and comments you post on an open job or under a bid, with your name.
  • Location: if you’re a PluggedIn worker, your precise location from the moment a Plug accepts your bid until you submit proof (on the way, on site and working), so that Plug can see you on a map with your arrival time. Sharing stops when you submit proof or the job ends. Your location is also used on your phone to show how far open jobs are; that is not stored.
  • Background checks (PluggedIn workers who choose one): your legal name, date of birth, email, phone number and current address, and your consent record. Your Social Security number is collected by the screening company on its own secure site, not by PluggedIn.
  • Device: a push notification token so we can send job updates.

AI features

PluggedIn uses AI to write job posts from your description, check jobs and chat messages against our rules, suggest bids, compare proof photos with what a Plug asked for, answer help questions, and summarize disputes and support requests for our team. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, most of this runs on your device. Otherwise, the job text, messages and photos needed for that task are sent to Google’s Gemini models through Google Firebase and Google Cloud, which process them to return a result under Google’s terms for those services. When a Plug posts a job video, PluggedIn makes subtitles from what is said in it using Apple's speech recognition, on your device when your device supports it, and uploads only the subtitle text with the video. AI never approves payment or makes account decisions on its own; people do. You can turn AI assistance off in Settings.

Location and time records

To show a Plug that their PluggedIn worker is present and working, we record when the PluggedIn worker checks in (with their distance from the job address and their selfie in a vest, and whether the Plug confirmed it matches their ID), whether their live location is at the job site while work is in progress, and when work starts and ends. These records are part of the job record used for payment and disputes.

What we don’t collect

We don’t receive your card number, bank account number or Social Security number. Apple Pay and Stripe handle payment details, and Stripe collects the bank and tax details needed to pay PluggedIn workers. We don’t use advertising trackers, and we don’t sell or rent your personal information.

How we use information

  • Run the marketplace: create your account, post and match jobs, process payments and payouts, show live job progress, and send notifications.
  • Keep people safe: verify age and identity, run background checks you request, investigate reports and disputes, and prevent fraud and off-platform payments.
  • Support: answer your questions and resolve problems.
  • Legal: meet tax, payment and legal obligations.

Your public resume

If you work jobs as a PluggedIn worker, PluggedIn builds a public resume from your completed jobs and the reviews Plugs leave you: your first name and last initial, face avatar, badges, star rating, reviews (with the reviewer's first name), jobs completed, hours worked, on-time rate, cancellations and short AI summaries of jobs. It never includes addresses, prices or messages. Anyone with the link can view it, and Plugs see it when you bid. Reviews you leave show your first name. Deleting your account deletes your resume.

What other users see

Other users see your first and last name as you entered it, your face avatar, ID-verified status, background-check badge (if cleared), rating, number of completed jobs, and your bids or job posts. While a job is open, its bids (the bidder's name, avatar, amount and note) and the public discussion under it are visible to everyone signed in to PluggedIn, so pricing stays transparent. Keep private details, like your exact address or phone number, in Messages. PluggedIn workers browsing open jobs see the neighborhood or city; the exact address is shared only with the PluggedIn worker who is matched to the job.

Who we share it with

• Google Firebase and Google Cloud (Google LLC) host our database, file storage, sign-in and notifications, and run the Gemini AI features described above. • Stripe, Inc. processes payments, refunds and payouts. • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) delivers email from courtney@praterapps.com, such as dispute case updates, using your email address and the message. • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, Apple Pay and push notifications. • A consumer reporting agency, only if you request a background check. • Law enforcement or others when required by law, or to protect someone’s safety. Each provider may only use your information to provide its service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you delete your account we delete your profile, avatar, full ID photo, open jobs and bids. We keep completed job and payment records, including check-in selfies, proof photos and dispute evidence, for up to 7 years for tax, accounting and dispute purposes, and background-check consent records as long as the law requires. Live location points are overwritten as new ones arrive and are not kept as a history.

Your choices

  • Location: you can turn off location access in iPhone Settings. PluggedIn workers then can’t share live progress, and Plugs will see status updates only.
  • Notifications: turn them off in iPhone Settings.
  • Access, correction and deletion: update your profile in the app, delete your account in Settings, or email us to request a copy of your information or a correction.
  • Michigan, California and other state residents may have additional rights; email us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored with Google Firebase. Access to the database is restricted by security rules so people can only read what they need for their own jobs. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you think your account was misused.

Children

PluggedIn is only for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we make important changes, we’ll update the date at the top of this page and let you know in the app.
