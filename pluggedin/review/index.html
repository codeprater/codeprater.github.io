PluggedIn — demo for App Review

One job from start to finish, in three parts. Parts 1 and 3 were recorded in the iPhone simulator. Part 2 is Apple Pay on a physical iPhone.

Part 1 — Posting a job, up to Apple Pay

Template, job photo, address, price and fee breakdown, the checklist, then the Buy with Apple Pay button.

Part 2 — Paying with Apple Pay (physical iPhone)

The Apple Pay sheet, confirming payment, and the payment held until the Plug approves the work.

Being added shortly.

Part 3 — After payment: the job through to payout

Payment held, the worker accepts and checks in, the Plug confirms it's them, proof is sent, the Plug approves and the payment is released, then the receipt and rating. This part uses the app's walkthrough mode with sample data.
