Background Checks

How the Background-checked PluggedIn worker program works, the FCRA disclosure, and your rights.

What a Background-checked PluggedIn worker is

PluggedIn workers can choose to complete a background check. PluggedIn workers who pass get a Background-checked badge and can take jobs that Plugs mark as Background-checked PluggedIn worker only. These jobs usually pay more.

How to start

In the app, go to Profile → Background check. Enter your legal name, date of birth, email, phone and current address, read the disclosure below, and give your authorization. The screening company will then email you a secure link to finish, including your Social Security number. PluggedIn never asks for your SSN in the app.

Disclosure regarding background investigation

PluggedIn may obtain a consumer report about you from a consumer reporting agency for the purpose of deciding whether you can take Background-checked PluggedIn worker jobs. The report may include identity verification, a Social Security number trace, national and county criminal records searches, and sex offender registry searches. This disclosure is provided in a document by itself, as the Fair Credit Reporting Act requires, and you will be given the name and contact details of the consumer reporting agency before the report is requested.

Your rights

You have rights under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), including the right to know what is in your file, to dispute incomplete or inaccurate information, and to receive notice before any adverse decision is made based on a report. Read “A Summary of Your Rights Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act” from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1022/k/

If something in the report is a concern

Before we decide not to clear you based on the report, we will send you a pre-adverse action notice with a copy of the report and the summary of your rights, and give you time to respond or dispute it with the screening company. If we then decide not to clear you, we will send an adverse action notice. You can still take jobs that don’t require a background check.

What Plugs should know

A background check is a point-in-time search of available records. It does not guarantee anyone’s identity, history, behavior or skill. Always use the safety tools in the app, and call 911 if anyone is in danger.

How long it takes

Most checks finish in 1–3 business days after you complete the screening company’s form. Your status shows in Profile → Background check.
