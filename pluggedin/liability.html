Please read carefully

This waiver affects your legal rights. By accepting it, you give up certain rights to sue PluggedIn.

1. Assumption of risk

I understand that jobs arranged through PluggedIn can involve physical work, lifting, tools, ladders, vehicles, weather, animals, and entering or allowing others into private property. These activities carry risks, including property damage, illness, injury, and in rare cases death. I choose to take part voluntarily, and I accept these risks, whether I am a Plug or a PluggedIn worker.

2. My responsibilities

I will only take or post jobs I can do safely and legally. I will describe hazards honestly, use proper equipment, follow safety laws, and stop if something is unsafe. As a PluggedIn worker, I will wear a high-visibility safety vest at all times on a job site. As a Plug, I am responsible for the condition of my property and for keeping people and pets safe during the job.

3. Release of claims

To the fullest extent the law allows, I release and agree not to sue PluggedIn, its owners, employees, and contractors for any injury, loss, damage, or claim arising from a job, from travel to or from a job, or from my contact with another user, including claims based on PluggedIn's ordinary negligence. This release does not cover gross negligence, willful misconduct, or anything the law does not allow to be waived.

4. Users are responsible for each other's conduct, not PluggedIn

Any claim I have about the work, behavior, or property of another user is between me and that user. PluggedIn may help through the dispute and support process, but is not responsible for what users do.

5. Insurance

PluggedIn does not provide health, disability, workers' compensation, auto, or liability insurance for users unless the app clearly says a specific job is covered. I am responsible for my own insurance.

6. Medical care

If I am hurt, I will seek medical care at my own cost. I allow emergency responders to treat me if I cannot consent myself.

7. Severability

If any part of this waiver is found unenforceable, the rest still applies to the fullest extent the law allows.

8. I understand

I am at least 18 years old. I have read this waiver, I understand that I am giving up substantial rights, and I accept it voluntarily.
