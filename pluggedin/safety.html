Safety on PluggedIn

How PluggedIn keeps Plugs and PluggedIn workers safe before, during and after a job.

On this page

  2. Everyone agrees on the work
  3. Know who's at your door

  5. Exact addresses stay private
  6. Payments stay in the app
  7. Cancel any time

Verified people

Every account is 18+ and verified with a government ID photo. PluggedIn workers can add a background check, shown as a badge on their profile and bids.

Everyone agrees on the work

Plugs must record a video or photos of the job before it can go out for bids. PluggedIn workers must agree to that exact task before starting, and send photo or video proof before payout.

Know who's at your door

You choose your PluggedIn worker from their profile, stars, reviews and work history. You see their face photo and name before they arrive.

Know they're there, and for how long

PluggedIn workers check in at the job address with a photo wearing a high-visibility vest. Plugs see when work started, how long it's been, the expected time and the finish-by time, and get alerts if the worker leaves the site or the job runs long.

Exact addresses stay private

PluggedIn workers browsing jobs see only the neighborhood or city. The exact address is shared with the one worker who is matched to the job.

Payments stay in the app

No cash, Venmo, Zelle or other off-app payments. Chat is checked automatically for payment requests and phone numbers. Off-app payment loses PluggedIn's refund and payment protection.

Cancel any time

Either side can cancel. Plugs can release a worker and the job goes back into the feed. Workers can give a job back.

Report, block and get help

Every job has Report, Block, Get help and a Safety center with a trusted contact. AI flags urgent safety requests for our team. If anyone is in danger, call 911 first.
