Hire help

Post it, pick a PluggedIn worker, watch it happen.

  • Start from a template or create your own, then record a video or photos of exactly what you want done.
  • Every PluggedIn worker is 18+ and ID verified. Choose Background-checked PluggedIn workers for premium jobs.
  • Follow your PluggedIn worker live on a map from “on my way” to done.
  • Your payment is held until you approve the photo proof.

Earn money

Find work nearby. Cash out when you want.

  • Swipe through open jobs, watch the Plug’s video, then bid or accept.
  • Wear a high-visibility vest on site and send photo or video proof when you’re done.
  • Keep 95% of the job price and 100% of tips.
  • Track every dollar in the money tracker, after fees.
  • Cash out to your bank, free, usually in 1–2 business days.
