    About Ahem.

    AHEM is for the moment a text is not enough.

    Record a short video in your own voice. Choose who needs to hear you. They get it full screen, with the words on screen, and a clear way to answer.

    HOME Tap AHEM and pick someone from your circle. Mom, Dad, a friend, or you.

    RECORD Hold the moment. Live captions appear while you talk. Flip the camera. Tap to stop.

    REVIEW AND SEND Play it back, choose who it is for, and send. Add other people when the same message should reach more than one person.

    FULL SCREEN The person you sent it to sees your video large, with the caption. They tap Got it, or they reply with an AHEM of their own.

    CUSTOMIZE Send now, in 10 minutes, in 1 hour, tonight, tomorrow, or pick a time. Add a caption if you want one.

    LOCK SCREEN An AHEM can show up on the Lock Screen with your name and the words that matter.

    REPLY They can answer with a video or a quick Got it.

    SCHEDULE Need it later? Choose a time that works.

    HISTORY Recent AHEMs and the people you send them to stay in one place.

    WIDGETS A tap on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, or watch face starts a new AHEM.

    AHEM is for personal messages and family coordination. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
