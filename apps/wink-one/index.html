    About Wink One

    WINK is note-passing for real life.

    Send a card to someone in the room or anywhere your share sheet can reach. iMessage, Mail, AirDrop, or Nearby.

    Pick an atmosphere. Stadium. Lounge. Daily Grind. Gen-Z. Caption a symbol, scribble it by hand, or paste a link. Hit Send.

    Ghost Mode when you’re done being found. One Shot when you only want a reply. Pin a place on the card if you want them to find you. Off by default.

    Made in Detroit. 18+
