    About PASSEE

    PASSEE makes greeting cards that move. Pick a design, type a name, and watch an envelope burst open with confetti, big bold words and your message then send it in Messages, Mail or anywhere you share.

    Make A Card In Seconds

    • Describe your card and on-device Apple Intelligence designs it: colors, words, party details and all.
    • Designs for birthdays, weddings, graduations, thank-yous, holidays and just because.
    • Big emoji and SF Symbols cards that burst out of the envelope.

    Make It Personal

    • AI photo collages from up to 9 of your photos.
    • Add an Apple Music song and choose the part that plays.
    • Put your own face on the card, or leave it off.
    • Add a gift note with Apple Cash, Venmo, Cash App or PayPal.

    Never Miss A Day

    • PASSEE finds upcoming birthdays and celebrations in your contacts and calendar.
    • Widgets show what's coming up and start a card with one tap.
    • Siri and Shortcuts can make a card for you.

    Everywhere You Are

    • iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.
    • iCloud keeps your cards in sync.
    • Friends without PASSEE open your card instantly with an App Clip.

    Private By Design

    Face cutouts, smart crops, AI designs and caption ideas are all made on your device. Contacts and Calendar are only read to find celebrations.
