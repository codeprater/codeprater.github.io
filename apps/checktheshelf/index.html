    About CHECKTHESHELF

    Before you buy it, check it.

    CHECKTHESHELF scans product labels, date codes, and lot numbers right from your camera and tells you instantly if something's safe to buy. No typing, no searching. Just point your camera and know.

    Open the scanner and it does the rest: the moment a label resolves, you get a clear result and a plain-language explanation, automatically. No extra taps.

    What It Does

    • Scans expiration dates, lot codes, and barcodes in real time
    • Flags items that match an active recall
    • Explains what a date code or lot number actually means, in plain English
    • Builds a searchable history of everything you've checked
    • Documents retail violations with photo and location evidence, ready to back up a report

    Built For Real Shopping

    CHECKTHESHELF was built in Detroit, for Detroit because knowing what's on the shelf shouldn't take a chemistry degree or a phone call to the manufacturer. Whether you're double-checking a sell-by date or documenting something that shouldn't be on the shelf at all, CHECKYASHELF gives you the answer on the spot.

    Private By Design

    Your scans and photos stay on your device. On-device Apple Intelligence powers the plain-language explanations — nothing is sent to a server to tell you what a label means.

    Learn As You Go

    The Learn tab breaks down date codes, lot numbers, recalls, and confusing label language with an "explain this more simply" option any time something still doesn't make sense.

    CHECKTHESHELF is a tool for shoppers, not a substitute for a recall notice from a retailer or manufacturer, and not legal advice.
