    About Hawt Potato

    Hawt Potato turns any moment into a fast, laugh-out-loud party game. Start a round, pass the potato, and keep moving before the timer runs out. With the App Clip, friends can join instantly no download required.

    Features:

    • Quick-play rounds with random timers
    • Instant App Clip invites for nearby friends
    • Custom player names and round lengths
    • Haptic and sound alerts
    • Score tracking and rematch button
    • Works offline for local play
