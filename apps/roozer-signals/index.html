    About ROOZER!

    ROOZER is a fast, expressive social app built for people who want to send energy, mood, and reaction without typing a full explanation.

    Instead of long messages, ROOZER lets users send curated emoji-based signals that capture hype, side-eye, affirmation, mood, exit energy, and everyday vibe. Each signal combines emoji, tone, and motion into a quick social shorthand that feels immediate, playful, and personal.

    ROOZER is organized around easy-to-browse categories, including Hype, React, Mood, Acknowledge, Vibe, Affirm, and Exit. Users can tap through a growing library of signals, send a random signal, explore the daily featured signal, and share visual signal cards.

    The app also includes arcade-style entertainment sections, Honeybadger and PRATER, which expand the experience with interactive game modes and challenge-based play.

    What you can do in ROOZER:

    • Send curated emoji-based signals instead of typing full messages
    • Browse signals by category
    • Discover the Signal of the Day
    • Use the random signal button for quick reactions
    • View and send signals from the full signal grid
    • Share custom signal cards
    • Track usage stats, streaks, and favorite patterns
    • Customize settings such as haptics and featured signal display

    Signal packs include:

    • Core signals
    • Street culture
    • Hand signs
    • Viral / internet slang
    • Faces and people
    • Animals and nature
    • Lifestyle / travel / objects
    • Zodiac
    • Symbols

    Arcade sections include:

    Honeybadger

    An arcade-style game area built for playful interaction and device-based challenges.

    Prater

    A multi-mode arcade section featuring fast, expressive mini-game experiences such as:

    • Hold Still
    • Don’t Flinch
    • Reaction Test
    • Staring Contest
    • Copy My Moves
    • Dot Rush
    • and other challenge-based modes

    Some arcade experiences may use supported device features such as camera, microphone, motion input, or face tracking depending on the game selected.

    ROOZER is designed for quick expression, social chemistry, and in-the-moment reactions. Whether you are sending a vibe, acknowledging a moment, hyping someone up, or jumping into arcade-style play, ROOZER turns communication and interaction into something more visual, immediate, and fun.
