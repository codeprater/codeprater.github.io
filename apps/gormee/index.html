    About GORMEE

    GORMEE helps you read and organize product labels, receipts, and agreements.

    • Scan a label or barcode, or type or paste text. GORMEE shows the details it can identify and files the report in My Items.
    • Scan a paper receipt, upload a photo, or import a PDF. GORMEE can surface a saved receipt when a later scan shares a product identifier.
    • Use Tools to review an agreement or organize details for a purchase issue. Reports show captured wording and available sources.
    • Keep track of dates you choose to save. Plain-language summaries are available, with optional Apple Intelligence explanations on supported devices.
