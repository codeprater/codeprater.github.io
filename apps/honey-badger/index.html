    About Honey Badger

    King Mamba drained the Great Hive dry. The bees fled, the rains stopped, and every animal on the savanna ran. Except one honey badger: Hudson.

    Honey Badger: The Last Hive is a cartoon action game in three parts: roam an open savanna, run side-scrolling missions, and brawl in stand-up, one-on-one fights. Now on the big screen.

    Hunt, Dig And Eat

    Explore three open boards: the Savanna, the Kalahari and Okavango, and the Serengeti. Chase down 18 kinds of prey, dig into termite mounds, raid beehives for honeycomb, and grab bird eggs for extra lives. What you eat matters: a big meal, bee larvae or a belly full of venom gives you a head start in your next fight.

    Watch Your Back

    Hudson's real-life enemies roam every board, and every one of them is far bigger than he is: brown hyenas, painted wild dogs, martial eagles, rock pythons, Cape buffalo and even elephants. They stalk, then they chase. Dive into a burrow to lose them, or turn around and stand your ground.

    Run The Missions

    Dive into burrows for side-scrolling levels full of quicksand, croc rivers, rock-throwing baboons, wildebeest stampedes and diving vultures. Find all three golden feathers in each mission and earn up to three stars.

    Win The Fights

    Every mission ends in a best-of-three brawl. Jab, power punch, roundhouse, sweep, bite and hang on, block, parry, and dig under attacks. Take hits to fill Hudson's Rage meter, then unleash it. Eight bosses each guard a piece of the stolen honeycomb, from Cackle the hyena to Prince Rax the lion, all the way to King Mamba himself at the Great Baobab.

    Get Stronger

    Spend honey on upgrades, climb seven ranks from Cub to Legend, and collect 15 trophies. Beat the King to unlock Badlands Mode: tougher bosses, double the honey.

    Play Your Way

    • Play with the Siri Remote, or connect a game controller for every move
    • Customize your controls: camera speed, invert camera, and swap A and B on controllers
    • Normal or Chill difficulty
    • Chirp, your honeyguide bird, gives tips you can turn down or off
    • Music and sound effect volume, big text, and an option to turn off flashing and screen shake
    • Your progress syncs through iCloud with your iPhone and iPad

    No ads. No in-app purchases. No data collected.

    New: Savage Edition

    Run the savanna as Hudson with the Siri Remote or a game controller: chomp bees, snarl, death-roll, play dead and out-stare TANK the lion.

    • 7 story chapters with boss fights against Cackle, The Catcher and TANK
    • Mini games, a daily challenge and streak rewards
    • A honey shop for new looks, upgrades and bait (earned in play, never bought)
    • Assist and One Move options so everyone can play
