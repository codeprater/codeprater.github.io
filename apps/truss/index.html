    About TRUSS

    TRUSS replaces the rented walkie-talkie with one app your whole crew already carries and runs the production day around it.

    Push-To-Talk That Just Works

    • Hold to talk on your channel, from the app, the Lock Screen, Apple Watch or your AirPods (press once to talk, again to send)
    • Live subtitles and translation, so everyone hears the channel in their own language
    • Every transmission is transcribed into a searchable radio log

    The Production Os

    • Today: your call time, meals, overtime and turnaround alerts before they cost money
    • Time clock with meal breaks and wrap, on iPhone, Apple Watch, Siri and the Action button
    • Payroll and timecards: straight time, 1.5x, 2x, meal penalties and invoices, exported to CSV
    • Live crew map: who's on set, at basecamp or away tap anyone to ping their phone and watch
    • Status dots on every name: online, clocked in, standby, busy, on break or offline

    One Live Feed

    Radio, messages, alerts, quick updates and clock-ins in one place. Long-press any message to react, reply by text or voice, or send your location.

    Safety

    • Incident alerts reach every phone on the channel full screen, with your location
    • Add notes and write a PDF incident report in seconds
    • Man-down check-ins and Apple Watch fall detection

    Ask Truss

    An on-device assistant that reads the radio log and the timecards together: "Did anyone say the truck moved?", "Who's on site?", "What did I miss?"

    Private By Design

    The radio log is encrypted with keys that stay on your devices. Location is only shared when you turn it on.
