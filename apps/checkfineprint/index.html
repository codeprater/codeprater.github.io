    About CHECKFINEPRINT

    Fine Print helps you read contracts and long forms before you sign. Scan pages with your iPhone, get on-device text recognition, and see plain-language explanations of clause patterns that often matter so you can ask better questions and know when to get professional help.

    What you can do

    Scan documents using Apple’s document camera (similar to scanning in Notes).

    Get a Signing checkpoint for each saved scan: a Low / Medium / High risk band, a best-guess contract type (heuristic), anonymized risk categories (labels and counts only), and a clause mix summary.

    Review Truth Cards and summaries that translate detected patterns into clearer language.

    Browse History and Learn topics to build familiarity with common terms (arbitration, data use, loans, real-estate language, and more).

    Use Siri & Shortcuts to jump to Learn or your last scan (local routing only).

    Adjust options in Settings: camera and Photos access, faster OCR, saving scans locally, compact summaries, optional notifications when a scan finishes, and more.

    Privacy Processing is designed to stay on your device. Fine Print does not upload your scanned images or OCR text to Fine Print servers for core features. Data you export with Share or copy is only sent where you choose.

    Important Fine Print is an educational tool. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Laws vary by place and situation. For decisions about signing, negotiating, or your rights, consult a qualified attorney.
