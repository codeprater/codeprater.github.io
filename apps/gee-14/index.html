    About GEE-14

    Don’t Flinch is a fast, addictive reflex-and-focus game built around one rule: stay in control. Compete in quick challenges that test steadiness, reaction speed, timing, and precision then track your progress with achievements, score cards, and unlock able style.

    Game Modes (6)

    • Hold Still Keep your hands steady until the timer hits the win mark.
    • Don’t Flinch Survive surprise FLINCH! checks. Any movement during the window ends the run.
    • Staring Contest Keep the phone close and stay steady. Break focus and you lose.
    • Simon Moves Record a motion sequence, then repeat it as accurately as possible.
    • Dot Rush Tap fast, keep energy alive, build combos, and chase high scores.
    • Reaction Test Tap only when the target appears. Early taps cost you. Slow taps end you.

    Progress + Rewards

    • Achievements System — Unlock trophies for wins, perfect runs, combos, streaks, and reaction speed.
    • Focus Points (FP) — Earn points each round based on performance and difficulty.
    • Win Streak Tracking — Stack consecutive wins for bragging rights (and achievements).

    Dot Rush Power-Ups

    Activate special boosts during play, including effects like time freeze, double points, slow motion, magnet-style assistance, and shield-style protection.

    Personalization

    • Themes — Switch your app look (Dark/Light + more unlockable styles).
    • Audio Packs + Volume Controls — Adjust SFX/music intensity to match your vibe.
    • Haptics + Motion Options — Tune feedback and motion sensitivity in Settings.

    Tips + Coaching

    Built-in Tips appear by mode to help you improve technique, timing, and consistency.

    Stat Cards + Sharing

    Every round can generate a Stat Card showing your result, time/score, FP, badges, and notes—saved on-device so you can review your history and share results.

    Sensor-Based Gameplay

    Don’t Flinch uses your device’s motion sensors (and proximity for specific modes) to detect steadiness and reaction performance.
