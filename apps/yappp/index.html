    About YAPPP

    YAPPP — Your AAC Personal Platform Plus

    Give voice to those who communicate differently. YAPPP is a free, modern AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) app designed for nonverbal and minimally verbal individuals, their families, caregivers, and speech-language pathologists.

    Communication Board

    • Tap tiles to speak words and phrases instantly with high-quality text-to-speech
    • Animated tiles with emoji, photos, and video support
    • Predictive suggestions that learn from usage patterns
    • Sentence Builder with grammar smoothing for natural-sounding speech
    • Customizable layouts: grid, list, or flow modes with adjustable tile sizes

    Asl Support

    • Long-press any tile to see the American Sign Language equivalent
    • AI-powered sign matching using natural language processing
    • Built-in vocabulary of common AAC signs

    Smart Camera

    • Point your camera to identify objects and create tiles instantly
    • Scan food labels for nutrition tracking with allergen alerts
    • Document scanning mode for quick text capture

    Caregiver Tools

    • Real-time caregiver feed with message history
    • Urgent message pinning and quick response options
    • Peer-to-peer device sync for multi-device households
    • Analytics dashboard with usage charts, vocabulary growth, and communication heatmaps
    • Export PDF reports, CSV data, and SLP-formatted summaries

    Works Everywhere

    • iPhone, iPad, Mac (Catalyst), Apple Watch, and CarPlay
    • AirPlay mirroring for classroom and therapy displays
    • Home Screen widgets and Live Activities
    • Dynamic Island support for active communication sessions
    • Siri Shortcuts for hands-free control
    • CloudKit backup and restore

    Accessibility First

    • VoiceOver compatible
    • Adjustable speech rate, language, and voice selection
    • Personal Voice support
    • Context-aware suggestions based on time and location
    • Visual schedules and notification reminders

    Completely Free

    YAPPP is and always will be free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads. Every person deserves a voice.

    Built with love for the AAC community.
