    About Au'commadate

    Au'commadate is a touch-only communication board and family support app. Built for nonspeaking and minimally verbal kids, teens, and adults, and for the parents, caregivers, and educators who support them.

    Speak On Tap

    Tap any tile to speak a phrase out loud. Hold a tile for a quiet menu: speak again, add to a sentence, pin it as a favorite, save an alternate phrasing, or edit the card. Build full sentences one tile at a time, clear them, speak them, start over.

    Your Board, Your Words

    Start with categories for core words, needs, feelings, requests, people, places, social moments, emergency, and time. Add your own cards. Pin favorites. Keep a "Mine" strip of phrases that belong only to you. Every edit is saved on this device, no account required to begin.

    Quick Cards

    One-screen cards you can show or share in the moment. Allergies, medications, contact info, communication style, accommodations, whatever you need on hand. Great for a new sitter, a clinician visit, an IEP meeting, or a substitute teacher.

    Routines

    Same-shape days, one step at a time. Build a morning routine, an after-school routine, a bedtime wind-down, then tap each step as it happens. Progress resets every day so tomorrow starts clean.

    Family Support Hub

    A calmer place for parents and caregivers: crisis lines and 988, nearby local help on a map, and quick access to topic areas like schools, transport, Medicaid, treatments, legal help, and parent peer support. Save the resources that matter and come back to them later.

    Privacy By Design

    Au'commadate runs entirely on your device. No tracking. No advertising. No account server. No microphone, no camera, no social features. Speech uses Apple's built-in text-to-speech. Export everything you have saved as a JSON file at any time, or delete it all in one tap from Settings.

    Accessibility

    Touch-only on purpose. Large tile targets, strong color contrast, VoiceOver labels across communication tiles, category chips, routine steps, and support cards, and full Dynamic Type support. No swipes or gestures to memorize, every action is a tap or a hold.

    For Whom

    Families of children with autism, Down syndrome, apraxia of speech, Rett syndrome, or other communication differences. Adults using AAC. Educators, speech-language pathologists, and caregivers who want a simple, private, daily-driver tool for communication and routine support.

    What You Get

    • A fully editable communication board with a sentence builder
    • Pinnable favorites and long-press actions on every tile
    • Custom quick cards you can show or share from the iOS share sheet
    • Daily routines with step-by-step progress
    • A 7-day outcomes snapshot so you can see real use over time
    • One-tap data export for clinicians, schools, or your own records
    • A curated hub of parent and caregiver resources
    • Crisis help and 988 always one tap away

    Au'commadate is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care. It is a daily communication and support tool designed to live alongside the clinicians, educators, and family members already in your corner.
