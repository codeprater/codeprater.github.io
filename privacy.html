Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Prater Apps LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the apps we publish on the Apple App Store and on this website.

What we collect

We design our apps to work on your device and to collect as little as possible. Each app's App Store page includes an Apple privacy label listing exactly what, if anything, that app collects. Where an app uses device features such as the camera, microphone, face tracking or motion sensors, it asks your permission first and uses that data only to make the feature work.

How we use information

We use information only to run and improve our apps and to respond to you when you contact us. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except where needed to provide the app (for example, Apple services such as iCloud or in-app purchases) or when required by law.

Children

Some of our apps are used by families and schools. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

Your choices

You can change app permissions at any time in your device's Settings. To ask about or delete information connected to you, email us.
