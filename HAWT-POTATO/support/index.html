How to play

  • Keep him alive. Don't let the potato die.
  • Home: PLAY mini-games or PASS A POTATO.
  • HAWT DROP: tilt the phone. Land in the green chute before the fuse hits zero.
  • PEEL!: your finger is the peeler. Scrape the skin off before he explodes.
  • DON'T GET MASHED!: drag him. Don't get hit.
  • Pass: you have it. One person: SEND. More people: tap a face. They have it: watch.
  • Close: long-press the potato, tap CLOSE.

What to include

  • What you were doing (Play, pass, Nearby, iMessage)
  • iPhone or iPad, and iOS version if you know it
  • Your display name in the app
  • For a player report: their display name and what happened

You can also email from the app: Settings → Email support.
