The game

Keep the potato alive. Play Hawt Drop, Peel!, and Don't Get Mashed on this phone. Pass a potato to friends. One potato has one holder. If you are holding it when the fuse hits zero, you are cooked. That is the game, not a defect. CLOSE ends a table so another game can start.

Your name

Safety

You can block and report other players. Do not use the app to harass people. We may restrict play that breaks these terms or Apple’s rules.

Nearby and Messages

Nearby uses Bluetooth and your local network while the app is open. Messages knocks someone to open HAWT POTATO. Play happens in the app.
